# Contributing to MoBetter

MoBetter is a personal project, shared as-is under the MIT license. It is **read-only**: pull requests and feature requests are not accepted.

**What is welcome:** a *Compatibility report* (Issues tab), telling me whether it works with your receiver. Never include your receiver's IP or MAC address, serial number, Wi-Fi name, PIN or any password.

You are free to fork it and change it for your own use.
